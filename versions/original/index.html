Postdoctoral researcher

I study how to recover hidden information from imperfect observations, and how to quantify what remains uncertain.

My work connects Bayesian inverse problems, partial differential equations, and computational methods, with a particular interest in experimental design and score-based diffusion models.

01 / Research

Inference, with uncertainty.

I work on the mathematical foundations and computation of inverse problems: from stability and approximation to practical sampling methods.
