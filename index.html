About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Mathematical Sciences at the University of Oulu, Finland.

My research concerns Bayesian inverse problems, uncertainty quantification, and partial differential equations. I am interested in the analysis and computation of these problems, including optimal experimental design and posterior sampling with score-based diffusion models.

I am the principal investigator of the project Optimal design of nonlinear inverse problems governed by partial differential equations, funded by the Magnus Ehrnrooth Foundation.

Research interests

  • Bayesian inverse problems and uncertainty quantification
  • Optimal experimental design
  • Diffusion models and posterior sampling
  • Nonlinear PDEs and mathematical modelling

See my selected publications or Google Scholar profile for more about my work.
